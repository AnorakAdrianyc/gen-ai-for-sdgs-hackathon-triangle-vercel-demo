Hong Kong Building Safety Desk

Risk map Stakeholder network Repair workflow

On 26 November 2025 a fire at Wang Fuk Court in Tai Po killed 168 people, including a firefighter, and injured 79. This desk does not score that estate. It shows sample buildings on a schematic 3D map, coloured low, medium, or high from a local data file, so owners’ corporations, managers, and residents can see how a risk picture would be read before a small defect is left alone.
